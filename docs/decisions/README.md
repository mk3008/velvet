# Decision Records

Record material implementation assumptions and choices here, with the reason, relevant Business Design and constraints, and consequences. Routine reversible technical choices do not require a record.

A record of an implementation choice is not approval of unresolved business policy. If a choice changes business meaning, responsibility, allowed states, units of work, or downstream guarantees without a basis in Business Design, leave it unresolved and request the responsible person's decision. Continue independent work where possible.
