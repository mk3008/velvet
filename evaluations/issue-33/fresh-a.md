In C2 the body comparison establishes grouping but does not alone persuade me that inspection improves after counting the caller facts. Another assessor could reasonably find a sufficiently concrete source walkthrough persuasive. The present frozen protocol already chooses a navigation experiment, so its unknown outcomes should not be presumed.
3. **Execution categories are incomplete.** A rejected candidate has no future execution trigger, while `wait` suggests one. Minimum clarification: permit `none` for REJECT and distinguish evidence collection now from runtime execution wait (C9). My summary uses the required now/wait/trial options with these qualifications.

Clarify only that pending verification of a specified invariant is different from an undefined invariant/interface. C9 is the latter. No rule revision is needed to defer it.
