## Ambiguities and minimum clarifications

1. v4 permits source comparison plus a concrete navigation explanation to suffice, while also saying names are hypotheses. The exact threshold for calling a newly named one-use DML unit a demonstrated navigation benefit remains judgmental. My C2 DEFER is because the diff demonstrates grouping and costs, while the claimed inspection advantage still depends on how the real task navigates the caller. A different reader could plausibly consider a concrete source walkthrough sufficient. Minimum clarification: “For navigation-only claims, name the specific facts/source inspection removed and added; a named block alone is insufficient. A comparative source walkthrough can supply this observation; reader trials are optional unless the protocol requires them.” This clarifies evidence content without requiring time measurement.
2. “Execution trial” can mean creating a fixture or evaluating one already created. Minimum clarification: record trial artifact state separately; in C2 the next trial action is comparison of the existing fixture, not another extraction.
3. Safety gaps can mean routine unexecuted readiness checks or unresolved effects that alter value. Minimum clarification: an established-value candidate can remain ADOPT pending routine gates; an unspecified failure contract that can introduce severe effects is DEFER until those effects and relevant costs are understood. C9 falls into the latter category.
4. The forced now/wait/trial vocabulary lacks “no implementation planned.” C7 uses wait only to fit the requested columns. Minimum clarification: allow “none” for REJECT, so the field does not suggest timing will revive it.

These are proposed clarifications, not tested revisions or reasons to rerun independent contexts for agreement.
