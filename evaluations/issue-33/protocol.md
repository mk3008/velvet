# Issue 33 protocol — frozen before trials

Baseline: `9f55499` (merged PR 32). Subject: Velvet only. Business meaning,
SQL, schema and external contracts are fixed. Issue 34 candidate-generation
research is separate. This pass may implement one justified private seam; it
need not implement a refactor to validate the action rule.

## Questions and bounded method

1. Inspect v3, Decision 0015, PR 32 and current A2/B2 records. Separate an
   unperformed benefit comparison from semantic uncertainty and timing.
2. Freeze a candidate proportional rule and context cases before independent
   applications. Two fresh contexts receive identical rule/cases/source evidence,
   no target answers or peer outputs. Record disposition, execution, evidence
   effort, decisive source and smallest missing observation. Preserve disagreement;
   do not rerun for agreement. Synthetic timing variations are explicitly
   counterfactual, not historical incidents.
3. Sketch B2, the private mutable UPDATE/DELETE execution/receipt seam, against
   keep. Preserve its caller guard, Work and retirement timing. Record exact
   interface and reversible source transformation. This is a trial artifact before
   any decision to ship it. A2 remains a distinct broader module candidate.
4. Compare comprehension on four fresh contexts: two baseline, two candidate,
   each sees only its assigned neutral sample. Ask for execution outline and
   the location/order of returned mutable key validation. Use an instrumented
   function reader to record expanded lines and calls. Permit full source when
   needed; count it. Score factual correctness before comparing inspection.
   Ground truth: pending/identity, source indexing, per-item admission/comparison,
   Work, guarded destination effects and retirement, completion/Processing;
   receipt checks follow DML and precede retirement; stable mapped-key guard is
   before reassessment and Work. Missing either timing must be visible in results.
   This measures one controlled navigation task, not time saved or general AI
   improvement. Full-file reading and name-guided reading can tie or regress.
5. If selected for runtime, retain exact-body reconstruction, relevant abnormal
   return/throw coverage, pnpm verify and PostgreSQL CI. A text equality check
   does not establish baseline correctness or database semantics.
6. Integrate only conclusions supported by these cases and source evidence;
   obtain independent Alder review. No automatic merge, new scoring framework,
   recurring audit, mandatory template or Alder change.

## Stop / rejection criteria

Stop after one paired rule application and four navigation trials expose the
boundary, and one scoped candidate is judged. Do not search for an optimum.
If independent answers diverge, document why and narrow claims; change the rule
only to address concrete ambiguity, labeling untested revisions. A local seam
with no observable task benefit or increased coordination may be rejected or
left unimplemented. Cheap rollback never licenses uncertain business semantics,
weakened assertions, missed SQL/transaction gates or unrelated urgent-fix edits.

## Evidence provenance

Protocol, rule and cases are committed before scored runs; record commit in
report. Agents are fresh contexts with procedural access restrictions, not OS
isolation. Prompts and full final responses are retained. Root scores against
above facts, reports line counts separately from judgment, and preserves all runs.
Ordinary decisions need only short reasoning; this research apparatus is not
imposed on routine changes.
